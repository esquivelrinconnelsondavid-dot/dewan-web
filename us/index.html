FAQ

Questions restaurant owners actually ask.

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?+
No. You keep the number your customers already know. We upgrade it to the official WhatsApp Business Platform — and in most cases you can keep using your WhatsApp Business app alongside Dewan during the transition.
Does it work with WhatsApp Business?+
Yes. Dewan runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform — Meta's professional-grade version of WhatsApp Business. Same number, same chats your customers know, with a full system behind it.
Is WhatsApp included in the price?+
Yes. Platform access and the operational messaging for your plan's order volume — menus, confirmations, status updates, tracking — are included in your monthly price.
Are there message limits?+
There are no hard message caps in day-to-day operation — plans are sized by order volume, and each includes the messaging a restaurant of that size normally needs. Marketing campaigns are separate and pre-approved by you. You won't find the word "unlimited" anywhere on this page: on the official platform every message has a real cost, and pretending otherwise is how other vendors surprise you later.
Do you charge commission on my sales?+
No. Never. You pay a flat monthly price. Whether an order is $15 or $500, your sale is 100% yours.
Can I use my own delivery drivers?+
Yes — that's the default setup. Your drivers get a simple app with the order, address, route and delivery fee, and you assign deliveries in one tap.
Can I keep using DoorDash / Uber Eats / Grubhub?+
Of course. Dewan runs your direct channel — the one where you keep 100% of the sale and own the customer relationship. Most restaurants keep third-party apps for discovery while moving their regulars to WhatsApp.
Can orders print automatically in the kitchen?+
Yes. The moment an order is confirmed, it prints on your thermal printer — exactly as the customer ordered it, no re-typing. An on-screen view is included too.
Can customers track their orders?+
Yes. Delivery orders get a live tracking link, and customers receive automatic status updates — which means far fewer "where's my order?" messages for your staff.
Do you set everything up?+
Yes, all of it: WhatsApp connection, menu, order flow, kitchen printing, delivery settings, user accounts, staff training and supervised test orders. Setup is a service, not a to-do list we hand you.
How long does implementation take?+
Typically 1–2 weeks from kickoff to your first live order — depending mostly on menu size and how quickly we get your information.
Can I have multiple locations?+
Yes. Each location runs its own WhatsApp line, menu and delivery setup. For multiple locations, talk to us about Enterprise pricing — and a consolidated multi-location dashboard is on our roadmap.
What happens if I get more orders than my plan?+
Your orders never stop. If you're consistently above your plan for two months in a row, we move you to the next plan — with notice, prorated. There are no per-order charges, ever. Outgrowing your plan is a good problem, and we won't punish it.
What happens with WhatsApp costs?+
Meta charges businesses per message on the official platform; your plan already covers normal operational usage for your order volume. If Meta significantly changes its fees, or your usage is far outside typical restaurant operation, we'll talk with you before anything changes on your bill. No surprises — that's the deal.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?+
Anytime, prorated, no penalties. Most restaurants start on Starter or Growth and move up as their direct orders grow.
